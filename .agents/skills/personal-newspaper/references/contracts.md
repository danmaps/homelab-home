# Source and edition contracts

The portable contract is `schema_version: 1`. Full-feature profiles also have `feature_version: 2`, generated by onboarding. The earlier three-page profile remains supported. Full fictional examples are `assets/full.profile.json`, `full.snapshot.json`, and `full.edition.json`; they establish no live facts or permissions.

## Profile and section plan

`title`, IANA `timezone`, `paper` (`Letter`/`A4`), ordered `sections`, selected `sources`, `members`, artwork/games/privacy/delivery/printing/schedule settings come from confirmed setup. The generated plan supports `front`, `planning`, `family`, `child`, `personal`, `discussion`, `reading`, and `games` kinds. Each section is one physical page. Family/child/personal/discussion modules are consecutive parts 1 and 2; child/personal/discussion pages carry `person_id`. The Family Desk precedes child pages. A shared reading `pair_group` identifies the consecutive organization/industry pair.

Sources have unique `id`, `required`, `max_age_minutes`, `coverage` (`selective` or `all_topics`) and `page_ids`. Optional `required_for_print` can differ from delivery requirements. All-topic sources need target page IDs and every collected topic must be cited on that pair. Collection still needs a body/topic checklist for material never imported.

## Snapshot and import envelope

A snapshot has `schema_version`, `mode: live|demo`, local edition `date`, `timezone`, and `sources` keyed by selected ID. A source import adds `source`, `date`, and `timezone` to one source record:

```json
{
  "schema_version": 1,
  "source": "notices",
  "date": "2026-10-24",
  "timezone": "Etc/UTC",
  "status": "partial",
  "complete": false,
  "method": "connector",
  "observed_at": "2026-10-24T07:00:00+00:00",
  "reason": "Actual scope checked and specific remaining limitations",
  "evidence": {"tool": "actual tool", "reference": "actual result or message ID", "scope": "exact folders, date range, pagination and bodies checked"},
  "items": [{"id": "notices:message-id:topic-id", "title": "Verified topic title", "summary": "Verified summary", "source_label": "Publisher and dated issue", "source_url": "https://example.org/original"}]
}
```

All item IDs are globally unique and stable. URLs use HTTP(S)/mailto or are absent for private sources. Retain issue/message/topic IDs, publication dates, original URLs, scope and source limits. Current means a complete live read using `connector`, `native_ui_verified`, `public_api`, or `web_fetch`, with actual evidence and a fresh offset-bearing observation. Caches/user-supplied reports retain original timestamps and limitations. Empty data proves an empty scope only after complete scope was checked. Unsupported or inaccessible sources are unavailable; capped/metadata-only/unknown-sync reads are partial.

The helper checks evidence structure and time consistency; it cannot prove that an observation happened. Recheck current coverage at delivery/printing according to the profile. Feed entries support index/title/description scope until originals are read. Never label unseen footage watched or invent full-article details. Preserve publisher attribution and applicable source reproduction limits.

## Normalized family records

Each individual item uses a confirmed `person_id`, never fuzzy name inference. Keep child-specific sender mappings and aliases in private setup. Each record still has the common title/summary/label/ID fields above.

| `kind` | Additional fields |
| --- | --- |
| `task_scope` | `person_id`, exact `lists`, `all_open: true`, `undated: true`, `nested: true`, `scope_complete: true` after actual complete inspection |
| `task` | `person_id`, `state: open|completed`, optional local `due_date`, `parent_id`, `notes`, `assignment_id` linking an overlapping school record |
| `grade_scope` | `person_id`, `all_latest: true` after all latest non-dropped published gradebooks were read |
| `grade` | `person_id`, exact `course`, `mark`, `percent`, and published `term_id`; preserve the term even if it differs from the current assignment quarter |
| `school_term` | `person_id`, real `term_id`, dated `start_date`/`end_date`; exactly one consistent current term must cover the edition date |
| `assignment` | `person_id`, `term_id` when recorded, local `due_date`, `state: open|missing|unscored|completed_late|completed|closed` |
| `lunch`, `shared_notice`, `opportunity` | Shared details belong once on the Family Desk; an opportunity does not imply enrollment/attendance |

Current assignments use the verified school term, with due dates as fallback only within verified boundaries when term ownership is absent. Keep a current-term future-dated missing flag distinct; do not infer completion from a date. Exclude previous-term assignments and linked TODO overlaps everywhere. An unknown term is an assignments coverage gap. Later completion evidence annotates the old source with `action_status: completed`; citing articles must preserve that status. It does not suppress future-cycle notices.

## Edition and visible rows

Edition fields are `schema_version`, `mode`, `title`, `date`, `timezone`, offset-bearing compilation `as_of`, and ordered `pages`. Values must agree across profile/snapshot/edition, and compilation falls on the edition's local date. Each page has its profile `id`, a `deck`, and `articles` (games pages may have no articles). Each article has `headline`, nonempty `paragraphs`, `source_ids`, and stable `fact_ids`. Optional kicker, table, steps and shared discussion question/decision are visibly rendered.

`grade_ids`, `assignment_ids` and `task_ids` list normalized source item IDs, also included in `source_ids`. The renderer builds exact visible table rows from those source records, rather than trusting a second transcription. Every latest published grade, current-term open/missing/unscored/completed-late assignment and every requested open task/subtask must have a visible row on the matching person's pair. Missing scope requires `coverage_notes: [{"module": "tasks|grades|assignments", "status": "partial|unavailable", "reason": "specific limitation"}]`. Such gaps prevent full-coverage acceptance/automatic printing.

A planning page includes `start_date`/`end_date`: tomorrow only; edition date through +6; edition date through +29. Every dated article falls inside its window. Planning articles use `audience: general|parent_action`; child-derived material requires a concrete `parent_action`. A discussion article requires `discussion_question` and `decision_needed`. The front uses `front_week` with Sunday-through-next-Sunday dates and cited, dated items. Weather and attention/calendar facts can be ordinary sourced articles, with optional visible tables.

Facts repeated across pages need `duplicate_exceptions: [{"fact_id": "...", "page_ids": ["..."], "reason": "..."}]`. Family-overview repeats and requested person/publication summaries are possible explicit exceptions. Revisited URLs/issues from earlier dates need `revisit_reasons` keyed by the URL/issue ID with the actual new development or an explicit evergreen rationale.

Artwork/comic/drawing/puzzle contracts are in [art and games](art-and-games.md). No unrequested advice letters or classifieds are generated.

## Output integrity

Each unused revision directory gets copied inputs and artwork, HTML, self-contained PDF, every page preview, `qa.json`, and `receipt.json`. Paper dimensions, page count, folios, embedded fonts, failed assets, basic boundaries and full-width image boxes are checked. Review records separate actual visual observations and preview/PDF/input hashes. QA cannot prove editorial completeness, authentic tools, real likenesses or physical delivery.

The receipt also stores edition ID, compilation/creation date, coverage, novelty, delivery and printing. Full live validation requires workspace history, including a real empty first-run history. Preserve final bytes and use new revision directories; never change receipts to bypass a failed check. Delivery and printing evidence use [operations](operations.md).
